2. Numerical comparison

Numerical comparisons are available for 18 of the 30 query-and-engine combinations. The figures increased in 8 cases, decreased in 9 cases and remained unchanged in 1 case. Another 12 combinations did not have a second numerical count.

The largest differences concern broad phrases. For instance, Google's estimated count for the Russian query about automating diploma and supplement preparation increased from 4,200,000 to 4,810,000, while the estimated count for the exact Russian master's-thesis topic fell from 240,000 to 46,300. Search result totals are approximate and depend on indexing, query interpretation and search engine behavior; they do not directly measure the number of scientific publications.

3. Interpretation

Broad search phrases typically return more pages than specialized queries that include Office Open XML, DOCX and XLSX. This supports combining exploratory and narrow searches while manually selecting trustworthy scientific and technical publications for the thesis overview, library and annotated links section.
