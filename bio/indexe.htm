Biography

Personal interests and development

My name is Anastasia Evgenyevna Baranova. I was born on 17 June 2003, and my academic and professional activities are now connected with Krasnodar and Kuban State University. I enjoy combining analytical work with creative activities. While my studies involve information systems, data and software design, in my free time I also like drawing and making things by hand. These seemingly different interests help me develop attention to detail, patience and a habit of bringing an idea to a finished result.

Drawing and handicrafts give me a chance to experiment with shapes, materials and visual arrangements. I appreciate the process of choosing an idea, planning the sequence of work and gradually refining its details. I notice a similar pattern in analytical projects, in which a large problem has to be divided into understandable elements before a reliable solution can be produced.

I also enjoy books about outer space and science fiction, particularly works related to the themes of Interstellar. Stories about space exploration encourage me to think about scientific curiosity, uncertainty, responsibility and the way people make decisions. Creative activities and reading give me a different perspective on the structured tasks I encounter during my studies.

Education and professional interests

From 2021 to 2025, I studied at the Faculty of Computer Technologies and Applied Mathematics of Kuban State University, completing a bachelor's degree in Mathematical Support and Administration of Information Systems. During those years, I developed an understanding of how mathematical methods, structured data and software systems are used to solve practical problems.

In 2025, I continued my education in the master's programme in Applied Mathematics and Computer Science at Kuban State University (2025–2027). I am particularly interested in business analysis, systems analysis, information systems design and quality assurance for electronic documents. These fields require a combination of technical knowledge and the ability to understand the needs of people who use the systems being developed.

My additional education includes the programme Data Processing and Information Systems Design, completed in 2023, and professional retraining in Digital Design during 2024–2025. Data modelling, careful documentation and interface design are complementary: a system should be logically correct while remaining understandable and convenient for its users.

Experience and practical projects

My practical experience is connected with the Department of Information Technologies at Kuban State University. I have worked with educational and administrative processes, helped clarify functional and non-functional requirements, and prepared technical documentation, process descriptions and instructions. I am interested in how well-designed automation can reduce repetitive operations and improve the reliability of routine work.

Some projects involved automating the preparation of student certificates and the printing of graduation documents. I also participated in the design of a university information system similar to an electronic gradebook. My contributions were related to modelling student data, academic performance records, coursework and graduation project records, and the relationships between students and academic supervisors.

I work with Excel, basic Power Query, and SQL queries involving selection, filtering, grouping and joins. I also develop skills in requirements management, diagrams, system documentation, UI/UX and the explanation of algorithms to users who may not have a technical background. I try to understand the actual problem before deciding on a particular software solution.

Master's research and plans

My master's research topic is Development of a Software Module for Detecting and Preventing Errors in the Preparation of Graduation Documents. It addresses the use of interrelated DOCX and XLSX files in university document workflows. The proposed approach combines file structure validation, data quality controls, cross-document consistency checks and rules defined by the specific academic process.

I am developing my scientific and educational communication skills as well. The individual section of this website contains a link to my Google Classroom course. My academic activity also includes a contribution to the proceedings of the 2026 Applied Mathematics Young Researchers Conference (PM2026). At present, I can provide a verified link to the official conference website, but not to a separately available online copy of my article.

Looking ahead, I would like to deepen my skills in business analysis, information systems design and software validation. I am particularly motivated by projects that help people work with information more clearly and consistently. At the same time, I intend to continue my creative activities and reading. I believe that maintaining interests beyond my primary field helps me approach complex problems with curiosity and flexibility.
